======================================================================
  SECTION 1 — FEATURE ENGINEERING A: REPAYMENT BEHAVIORAL (Last 3 Installments)
======================================================================

  Rationale:
  Payment behaviour in the last 3 months is the strongest near-term
  predictor of the next-month DPD.  Stress signals appear ~3 months
  before default (per the data dictionary design), so features built
  over a 3-installment rolling window capture the deterioration
  trajectory that precedes delinquency.

  Repayment features built in 0.8s
  Observation rows (raw) : 1,048,575

======================================================================
  SECTION 2 — FEATURE ENGINEERING B: BANK BEHAVIORAL SIGNALS (Last 3 Months)
======================================================================

  Rationale:
  Bank-account behavioral signals (balance volatility, cash-flow
  consistency, income/spending shocks) are LEADING indicators of
  financial stress — they deteriorate 1-3 months BEFORE payment
  behaviour deteriorates.  The combination of income_shock_flag +
  balance_volatility > 0.5 is explicitly cited in the data
  dictionary as the strongest default predictor.

  Behavioral features built in 1.5s
  Behavioral rows         : 1,236,752

======================================================================
  SECTION 3 — FEATURE ENGINEERING C: ORIGINATION FEATURES (Loans × Customers)
======================================================================

  Rationale:
  Origination-time features capture the structural creditworthiness
  of the borrower.  While they do not change over time, they provide
  the baseline risk level against which behavioral deterioration is
  measured.  Bureau score, risk tier, and employment stability are
  the strongest IV-ranked predictors from the EDA.

  Origination features: 18 variables

  SECTION 5 — TRAIN / TEST SPLIT

======================================================================
  SECTION 6 — MODEL TRAINING: GradientBoostingClassifier
======================================================================

  Algorithm: sklearn GradientBoostingClassifier
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────
  Choice rationale:
  • Sequential boosting builds an additive model of decision trees,
    each correcting the residuals of the previous — ideal for
    tabular credit data with mixed feature types and non-linear
    interactions (e.g. income_shock × balance_volatility).
  • Naturally handles mixed numeric features (no scaling required).
  • Produces well-calibrated probabilities for risk-bucket thresholding.
  • Supports SHAP TreeExplainer for fast, exact feature attribution.

  Hyperparameters chosen via domain knowledge and prior CV results:
  • n_estimators=300  : enough trees for convergence without over-fit
  • learning_rate=0.05: low LR + more trees → better generalisation
  • max_depth=4        : shallow enough to avoid over-fitting on noisy
                         behavioral signals
  • subsample=0.80    : stochastic boosting reduces variance
  • min_samples_leaf=30: minimum leaf size prevents over-fitting small
                         minority-class clusters

  Training time : 249.7s
  Optimal threshold (F1-max on train): 0.556

======================================================================
  SECTION 9 — RISK SCORECARD: GREEN / AMBER / RED BUCKETS
======================================================================

  Bucket construction:
  Predicted probability P(30+ DPD in next 30 days) is converted
  into three actionable risk buckets calibrated to operational
  decision thresholds:

  🟢 GREEN  : P < 0.15  →  Low risk — standard monitoring
  🟡 AMBER  : 0.15 ≤ P < 0.40  →  Medium risk — proactive outreach
  🔴 RED    : P ≥ 0.40  →  High risk — collections escalation

  Thresholds chosen to:
  • Green captures the bulk of truly performing loans (high specificity)
  • Amber is the proactive intervention zone — catch loans before they tip
  • Red triggers immediate collections action (minimum 1 wasted contact)
